GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, Answered

Where do you operate?

We cover all of East London, and mostly work in Tower Hamlets, Hackney and Newham. Tell us your event location when you enquire.

What drinks do you serve?

Matcha, iced lattes, Spanish lattes and Matcha Spanish (made with sweetened condensed milk), plus mint and strawberry mojitos. Our menu is alcohol-free.

What events do you cater for?

Weddings, corporate gatherings, brand activations and private celebrations, plus the occasional venue event.

How much does it cost?

Every event is quoted individually. The price depends on the number of guests, how far we have to travel and how you want to pay. Menu prices start at £3.50 per drink, and we are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.

Can I pay per drink, or have unlimited drinks?

Both are possible. You can pay for each drink, or choose unlimited drinks until our stock runs out. Tell us which you prefer when you enquire.

How far in advance should I book?

Please book at least one month in advance.

How do I book?

Send your date, location and guest count by email (info@drinksmadness.com) or WhatsApp and we will come back to you with a quote.

Can you cater for allergies?

Please tell us about any allergies or dietary requirements when you book. Full ingredient lists for our matcha, lattes and syrups are available on request.
