OUR STORY

About Drinks Madness

Drinks Madness is an exclusive mobile drinks bar based in East London, dedicated to bringing premium handcrafted beverages to private events and celebrations.

From smooth matcha and iced lattes to vividly refreshing mojitos, we ensure your guests are served something they will actually remember.

Drinks Madness was started by a former barista who loved the work, and loved how a good drink helps people get their day off to a good start. So we decided to make our own, our own way.

One day we would like to open a shop, and we will still take the occasional venue event. For our latest events, follow us on Instagram or TikTok.

Curated For Events

We provide the drinks, the aesthetic setup, and the madness. Whether you are hosting a corporate gathering, a private wedding, or a brand activation, our team is equipped to deliver a flawless beverage experience.
