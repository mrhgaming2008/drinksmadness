Privacy Policy & Legal Notice

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Data Protection (UK GDPR)

Drinks Madness ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. When you contact us via email (info@drinksmadness.com) or WhatsApp to book mobile bar services, we collect personal data such as your name, contact number, event date, and location. This information is used strictly to fulfil your booking enquiries and is never sold to third parties.

2. Statutory Business Disclosure

In compliance with the UK Companies (Trading Disclosures) Regulations 2008, our mobile bar service operates in East London, UK. Official correspondence can be directed to our booking email.

3. Pricing & Terms

All menu pricing is displayed in GBP (£). Drinks Madness is not currently VAT registered, therefore no VAT is added to your bill or displayed in our pricing. Mobile event bookings are subject to custom quotation based on guest count, location, and minimum spends agreed upon prior to confirmation.

4. Allergen & Food Information

In accordance with the Food Information Regulations 2014, full ingredient lists for our matcha, lattes, and syrups are available upon request. Customers must notify us of any specific dietary requirements or allergies prior to event service.

5. Cookies & Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics (provided by Google) to understand how visitors use this website, such as which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are located, and what type of device they use. This uses cookies and sends anonymised usage data to Google. Analytics is switched off unless you click Accept on the cookie banner, and no analytics cookies are set if you decline. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie Settings" in the footer.
