BRAND ACTIVATIONS

A Drinks Bar for Your Brand Activation in East London

Give your brand moment a drinks bar people will want to stop at. Drinks Madness brings handcrafted matcha, iced lattes, Spanish lattes and mojitos, plus an aesthetic setup, to brand activations across East London.

A good drink is an easy reason for people to join in. We provide the drinks, the setup and the madness, and you tell us the date, the place and how many people you expect.

What we bring to your activation

The drinks

Matcha, iced lattes, Spanish lattes and mint and strawberry mojitos. The menu is alcohol-free.

The setup

We provide the drinks, the aesthetic setup and the madness.

The booking

Bookings are made at least one month in advance, with a quote based on your event.

How booking works

1. Tell us the details

Send your date, location and guest count.

2. Get your quote

Custom pricing based on guest numbers, distance and how you pay.

3. We bring the madness

Once agreed, we bring the drinks and the setup.

Pricing is custom. You can pay for each drink, or choose unlimited drinks until our stock runs out. See how the two options work. We cover all of East London and mostly work in Tower Hamlets, Hackney and Newham.

Questions about brand activations

How do I get a quote for an activation?

Send us your date, location and expected guest count. Pricing is custom, based on guest numbers, distance and whether you pay per drink or go unlimited.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover all of East London and mostly work in Tower Hamlets, Hackney and Newham.
